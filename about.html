KCC is a Catholic learning community serving students in Kabankalan and nearby communities. We believe education is more than gaining knowledge: it is also a chance to build character, discover your gifts, and learn how to serve.

Our aim is to create an environment where students feel encouraged to ask questions, work toward their goals, and treat others with dignity.

Our Compass

Vision & Mission

The vision and mission that guide the KCC community.

Our Vision

Building the future, together.

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

Our Mission

Education rooted in the Gospel.

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

What We Stand For

The Four Pillars

Four commitments shape learning and life at Kabankalan Catholic College.

I

Academic Excellence

Rigorous academics that challenge students to think critically and develop a genuine love for learning.

II

Faith Formation

Deepening faith through Catholic tradition, prayer, and a personal relationship with God.

III

Community Service

Reaching out to those in need, putting the Gospel into practice through hands-on service.

IV

Holistic Development

Shaping students who are not only skilled and knowledgeable but also grounded in strong values.
