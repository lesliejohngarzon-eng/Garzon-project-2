Learning with purpose

Every student has
a story still unfolding.

KCC is a Catholic learning community serving students in Kabankalan and nearby communities. We believe education is more than gaining knowledge: it is also a chance to build character, discover your gifts, and learn how to serve.

Our aim is to create an environment where students feel encouraged to ask questions, work toward their goals, and treat others with dignity.
