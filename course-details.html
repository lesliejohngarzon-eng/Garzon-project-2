A useful place to start

Look beyond the
program title.

Think about the subjects that hold your attention, the kinds of problems you enjoy solving, and the work you might like to do in the future. Then compare those interests with the official curriculum and entry requirements.

Questions worth asking

  • What subjects and practical experiences are included?
  • What are the admission requirements and deadlines?
  • What materials or documents should I prepare?
  • Who can I speak with if I need help deciding?
Important: This page is a general guide, not an official program prospectus. Confirm exact program names, curriculum, fees, and admissions information with KCC.
